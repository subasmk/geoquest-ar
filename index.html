GeoQuest AR

Find hidden treasure in the real world. Walk, look, answer, win.

How to play
  1. Go outside to an open area (school ground) and use Chrome on Android.
  2. Allow camera, location and motion.
  3. Hold the phone up and turn around. Gold treasures float in the world.
  4. Walk to a treasure (within 5 m) to grab it, then answer the question.
  5. Find all 5 as fast as you can.
Team Techneon - Problem #2 - Demo build. Phone GPS is about 3-5 m accurate; a GNSS receiver with CORS data would make it centimetre-level.
